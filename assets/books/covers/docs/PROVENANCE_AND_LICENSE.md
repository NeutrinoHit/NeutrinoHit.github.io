# Provenance and licensing notes

- Deliverable artwork and covers are SVG/vector compositions with no embedded raster images.
- No font files are included.
- The statistical-methods illustration and procedural JUNO-like detector are
  original code-generated diagrams, not traces or embedded copies of published
  figures.
- The NeutrinoHit globe uses Natural Earth 1:110m land geometry. Natural Earth
  data is public domain.
- The neutrino, particle-physics and gravity illustrations are original,
  deterministic diagrams generated by `source/generate_series.py`.
- The v2 neutrino illustration is an original deterministic SVG sketch made
  from code. Two user-supplied images informed only the high-level composition:
  a magnetically structured jet and an edge-on, strongly lensed black-hole silhouette.
  The reference rasters are neither embedded, distributed, nor traced.
  Its revised light arcs, disk filaments and exterior jet funnel are generated
  by `source/black_hole_sketch.py`. Qualitative distinctions between disk images,
  shadow and photon ring follow [NASA's explanation](https://www.nasa.gov/universe/nasa-visualization-shows-a-black-holes-warped-world/);
  no NASA image geometry or simulation data is reproduced.
- The Baikal-GVD vignette is a procedural vector sketch from
  `source/baikal_gvd_sketch.py`. Its basic eight-string cluster geometry follows
  the collaboration's [telescope description](https://baikalgvd.jinr.ru/telescope/)
  and [JINR's 2026 expedition report](https://dlnp.jinr.ru/en/news/3779).
  Optical-module hits and light intensity are illustrative; no measured event,
  photograph, or external graphic is reproduced.
- The particle-physics event display is explicitly schematic and is not
  experimental data.
- The eight Neutrino Sky Atlas objects are original vector constructions in
  `source/neutrino_constellations.py` and `source/neutrino_atlas_motifs.py`,
  not traced constellations or copied charts.
  The unitarity triangle follows the mathematical construction in
  [PDG, section 14.7.3](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2023/reviews/rpp2022-rev-neutrino-mixing.pdf).
  Parameters are inherited from the series' illustrative vacuum model; star
  positions are fictional. Relic mist, anomaly contours, hierarchy scales,
  Majorana cluster, CP bridge and BSM vortex use original procedural geometry,
  not observational maps. Atlas labels use the locally available Cochin Italic
  face as live text in the editable source and as outlines in the published
  SVG/PDF exports; no font file is distributed.
- Numerical coefficients and physics parameters for the JUNO-like calculation
  are documented in `STATISTICAL_METHODS_REFERENCES.md` and `AUDIT.md`.
